Using UCC Spaces

UCC Spaces is provided by the University of Cape Coast for finding and requesting classrooms on campus. By creating an account or making a request you agree to these terms.

Accounts

  • Use your own name and a university email address where possible.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity under your account.
  • Do not share an account or create accounts for other people.
  • Staff roles are granted only after verification by the timetable office.

Bookings

  • Rooms are booked in whole-hour periods between 06:30 and 20:30 by default, Africa/Accra time. The timetable office may change the daily window.
  • Student requests are confirmed only when the timetable office approves them. A pending request holds the room until it is decided.
  • One person cannot hold two rooms at the same time, and a room cannot hold two bookings at the same time.
  • Cancel a booking you no longer need so the room becomes available to others.
  • The timetable office may reject a request, or close a room temporarily for maintenance, examinations or university events.

Acceptable use

Do not attempt to book rooms you do not intend to use, submit false details, interfere with the service, or access another person's account or data. Accounts may be suspended for misuse.

Availability

The service is provided as-is. Room details, capacities and facilities must be confirmed with the timetable office. The university is not liable for a booking that is refused, cancelled or changed under these terms or for interruptions to the service.

Contact
