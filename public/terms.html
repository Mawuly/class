Using UniGuide

UniGuide is provided for students and staff of the University of Cape Coast to find, book and check in to lecture rooms and auditoriums on campus. By creating an account, booking a room or checking in you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

Accounts

  • Use your own name and your university email address where possible. You can also sign in with Google.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity under your account.
  • Do not share an account or create accounts for other people.
  • If you sign in with Google using the email address of an existing account, the two are joined and the old password stops working; keep using Google, or set a new password with "Forgot password".
  • Lecturer and timetable office access is granted only after the timetable office has checked your offer or appointment letter.

Bookings

  • Bookings start on the hour or half hour and last whole hours. Lecture rooms can be booked between 06:30 and 20:30 by default, for up to 4 hours. Auditoriums (event rooms) can be booked from 06:30 until 05:00 the next morning, for up to 12 hours. Times are Ghana time (Africa/Accra). The timetable office may change these limits.
  • Bookings can be made up to 150 days ahead. Lecturers and the timetable office can book weekly.
  • Students can book rooms open to everyone, for study, meetings or events. Depending on the timetable office's setting, a student booking is confirmed straight away or waits for approval; a request waiting for approval holds the room until it is decided. A rejection always comes with a reason.
  • One person cannot hold two rooms at the same time, and a room cannot hold two bookings at the same time.
  • The teaching timetable is published by the timetable office and shows as booked. The timetable office may also close a room for a time for maintenance, examinations or university events, and may cancel bookings when a room must be withdrawn; you will be told.
  • Cancel a booking you no longer need, or check out when you leave early, so the room becomes available to others.

Checking in

  • Check in at the room's door by scanning its QR code or typing the code on the sign, from 10 minutes before your booking starts until 15 minutes after.
  • If nobody checks in by then, the booking is released and the room is shown as free for others. This applies to timetable lectures too.
  • For a timetable class, anyone attending (course rep, lecturer or student) may check the class in. Check a class in only when it is actually taking place in that room.
  • While the timetable office requires it, check-in works only when your phone shares its location and shows you are at the room's building.

Acceptable use

Do not book rooms you do not intend to use, submit false details, check in for a booking or class you are not at, share door codes or photographs of door signs so others can check in remotely, fake your phone's location, interfere with the service, or access another person's account or data. The timetable office may cancel bookings and suspend accounts for misuse.

Availability

The service is provided as-is. Room names, capacities, facilities and building locations should be confirmed with the timetable office, and walking directions are provided by Google Maps. The university is not liable for a booking that is refused, cancelled, released or changed under these terms, for a check-in refused because of an inaccurate phone location, or for interruptions to the service.

Changes

These terms and the privacy policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated terms.

Contact
