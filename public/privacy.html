Privacy policy

University of Cape Coast · Last updated 15 November 2026

What this service is

UniGuide helps students and staff find free classrooms in the Sandwich Lecture Theatre, the New Lecture Theatre and the C. A. Ackah Theatre, and send booking requests to the timetable office.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and optionally your index number, department and phone number.
  • Booking details: the room, date, time, course or activity, course code, supervisor and any note you add.
  • Technical records: sign-in sessions, password-reset attempts, and an audit record of booking and administrative actions.

Why we use it

To create and secure your account, check room availability, route requests to the timetable office, prevent double bookings and misuse, and keep an accurate record of room use. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

You can see your own bookings. Timetable office staff can see requests, account details needed to decide them, and the audit log. Other users see only whether a room is free, not who booked it.

Storage and retention

Accounts and bookings are stored in the university's booking database. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and reset codes are stored only as hashes. Audit entries are append-only. Records are kept for as long as the account is active and for the period required by university policy.

Preview mode

When the site is opened as a local preview without the booking database, accounts, bookings and closures are saved only in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to the university. Clearing site data or selecting "Reset bookings" removes them.

Cookies

A single session cookie keeps you signed in. It is required for booking and administration and is removed when you sign out or it expires.

Your choices

You may cancel a booking, correct your details, or ask the timetable office to close your account and delete your personal data, subject to record-keeping requirements. Password reset is available from the sign-in page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: the Timetable Office, University of Cape Coast.
