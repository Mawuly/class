Privacy policy

UniGuide · University of Cape Coast · Last updated 18 November 2026

What this service is

UniGuide helps students and staff at the University of Cape Coast find free lecture rooms and auditoriums, book them, get walking directions to them, and check in at the door. The timetable office uses it to publish the teaching timetable, manage rooms and approve requests. This policy explains what personal information UniGuide uses and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and, if you add them, your index number, department and phone number. Your phone number is used only for password-reset codes, after you confirm it with a code sent to it.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your name, your email address and a permanent Google account number, so we can find your account next time. We never see your Google password, and we do not get access to your contacts, emails or files.
  • Staff verification: if you ask for lecturer or timetable office access, your staff ID, position, department and the offer or appointment letter you upload. The letter is deleted once the timetable office has decided; only a fingerprint of the file (a SHA-256 hash) is kept to show which letter was checked.
  • Bookings: the room, date and times, what it is for, course code, supervisor, number of people and any note you add, plus whether it was approved, cancelled, checked in, checked out or released.
  • Check-in: when and how you checked in (QR code or typed door code) and, for a timetable class, which person checked the class in.
  • Location at check-in: see "Location" below.
  • Technical records: your sign-in session, password-reset and phone-confirmation attempts, and an audit log of sign-ins, bookings and administrative actions, including the IP address they came from.

Location

UniGuide asks your phone for its location only when you check in to a booking, and only to see whether you are at the room's building. Your phone's position is compared with the building's position and then discarded: we never store your coordinates. We keep only a label (at the building, away from it, location not shared, or building location not set) and, when you were away, the distance in metres and how precise your phone said it was. If you do not share your location, or it is too imprecise, check-in may be refused while the timetable office requires location; the message tells you why. UniGuide never tracks your location in the background.

Why we use it

To create and secure your account; to show which rooms are free and prevent double bookings; to confirm that rooms are actually used and free up rooms nobody turned up for; to let the timetable office decide requests, verify staff and plan room use; to send password-reset codes; and to keep an accurate record of room use and administrative decisions. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • You see your own account, bookings and notifications.
  • Other people see whether a room is free or booked. When signed in, they can also see the title and course code of bookings on the room's door page and day board, and the lecturer named on the timetable, but not who booked a room.
  • The timetable office sees requests and the account details needed to decide them, staff verification requests and letters (until deleted), statistics, check-ins flagged by the location check (with the person's name and the distance), and the audit log.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts the database where accounts and bookings are stored.
  • Vercel hosts the website and its server.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Arkesel sends text messages with password-reset and phone-confirmation codes; it receives your phone number and the message.
  • An email provider sends password-reset codes by email; it receives your email address and the message.
  • Google Maps: the "Directions" links open Google Maps with the building as the destination. UniGuide does not send your location to Google; Google Maps uses it under Google's own privacy policy if you allow it.

Storage and retention

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Reset and phone codes are stored only as hashes, expire after 10 minutes and can be tried at most five times. Calendar subscription links are stored only as hashes. Staff letters are deleted after the decision. Audit entries cannot be changed or deleted. Accounts and bookings are kept for as long as the account is active and for the period required by university policy.

Cookies and browser storage

A session cookie keeps you signed in; it is removed when you sign out or after eight hours. During "Continue with Google" a second cookie lasts at most ten minutes, to check that the answer from Google belongs to the sign-in you started. These cookies are needed for the service to work and are not used for tracking. The interface preview page keeps its sample data only in your browser's local storage.

Your choices

You may cancel a booking, remove your phone number, correct your details, or ask the timetable office to close your account and delete your personal data, subject to the university's record-keeping requirements. You can refuse to share your location; check-in may then be unavailable while location is required. Password reset is available from the sign-in page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: the Timetable Office, University of Cape Coast.
