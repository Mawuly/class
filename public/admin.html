    When rooms can be booked

    Applies to new bookings, the free-room search, the day board and door pages straight away. Bookings already made outside the new times are kept. Temporary closures are on the Rooms page.

    Student requests

    Students can still only book rooms open to everyone, for study, meetings or events. Bookings nobody checks in to are released after the grace period either way. Requests already waiting stay in Approvals.

    Check-in location

    Applies to QR-code and typed-code check-ins. With "required", people must allow location on their phone and be at the building. Buildings without a location are never refused.

    People

    People register themselves and start as students. Make lecturers and admins here.
